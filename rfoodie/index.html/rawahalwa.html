1.Roast semolina
Heat ghee in a pan, add rawa, and roast on low flame until golden and aromatic.
2.Prepare syrup
In another pan, boil water (or milk). Add sugar and cardamom powder, stir until dissolved.
3.Combine
Slowly pour the hot syrup into the roasted rawa, stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
4.Cook
Keep stirring until the mixture thickens and the ghee starts to separate.
5.Garnish
Fry cashews and raisins in a little ghee, add to halwa, mix well, and serve hot.
