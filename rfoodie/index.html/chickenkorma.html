1.Prepare base
Heat oil/ghee in a pan. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
2.Add aromatics
Stir in ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
3.Cook chicken
Add chicken pieces, turmeric, coriander powder, and red chili powder. Sauté until chicken is lightly browned.
4.Add tomatoes & yogurt
Mix in tomato puree and whisked yogurt. Cook on medium heat until oil separates.
5.Spice it up
Add garam masala, cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves. Stir well.
6.Make it creamy
Add cashew paste if using, and simmer for 15–20 minutes until chicken is tender.
7.Steam (Dum)
Cover tightly. Cook on very low heat for 20–25 minutes to let flavors meld.
8.Finish & serve
Adjust salt, garnish with fresh coriander, and serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.
