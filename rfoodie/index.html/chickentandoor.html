1.Prepare marinade
In a large bowl, mix yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin, coriander, mustard oil, and salt.
2.Marinate chicken
Make small cuts in the chicken pieces, coat them well with the marinade, cover, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours (overnight for best flavor).
3.Preheat oven/tandoor
Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F) or prepare a traditional tandoor/grill.
4.Cook chicken
Place marinated chicken on a rack or skewers. Roast/grill for 30–40 minutes, turning occasionally, until cooked through and slightly charred.
5.Serve
Garnish with lemon wedges, onion rings, and fresh coriander. Serve hot with naan or rice.
