1.Prepare mixture
In a large bowl, combine minced beef, onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, coriander leaves, and all spices. Mix thoroughly.
2.Shape kababs
Divide the mixture into equal portions and shape into long seekh kababs or round patties.
3.Preheat grill
Heat a grill or tandoor. Brush kababs lightly with oil or ghee.
4.Cook kababs
Grill kababs on medium-high heat for 20–25 minutes, turning occasionally until evenly cooked and slightly charred.
5.Serve
Serve hot with mint chutney, lemon wedges, and naan or rice.
