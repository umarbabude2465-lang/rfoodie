1.Prepare cashew paste
Soak cashews in warm water for 15 minutes, then grind into a smooth paste.
2.Cook onions and tomatoes
Heat ghee in a pan, sauté onions until golden, add tomatoes and cook until soft.
3.Add spices
Mix in ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
4.Blend gravy
Cool the mixture slightly, then blend into a smooth paste.
5.Cook gravy
Return paste to pan, add cashew paste, milk, and salt. Simmer for 5–7 minutes.
6.Add paneer
Add paneer cubes and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in cream and garam masala.
7.Garnish and serve
Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot with naan or rice.
