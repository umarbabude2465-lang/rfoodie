1.Wash and soak rice
Wash basmati rice until water runs clear. Soak for 30 minutes, then drain.
2.Cook beef
Heat oil in a heavy pot. Add whole spices (bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, peppercorns). Toast for 1 minute. Add onions and sauté until golden. Stir in ginger and garlic paste, then add beef pieces. Fry until browned.
3.Add spices and yogurt
Mix in turmeric, red chili, coriander, cumin, and salt. Add tomatoes and cook until softened. Stir in yogurt and cook until oil separates from masala.
4.Simmer
Add 2–3 cups water, cover, and simmer until beef is tender (about 45–60 minutes).
5.Parboil rice
In another pot, boil water with salt. Add soaked rice and cook until 70% done. Drain and set aside.
6.Layer biryani
In a large pot, layer half the rice, then spread beef curry over it. Top with remaining rice. Sprinkle garam masala, mint, and coriander leaves.
7.Steam (Dum)
Cover tightly. Cook on very low heat for 20–25 minutes to let flavors meld.
8.Serve
Gently mix layers before serving. Garnish with fried onions if desired. Serve hot with raita or salad.
