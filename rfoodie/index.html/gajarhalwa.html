1.Cook carrots
Heat ghee in a heavy pan, add grated carrots, and sauté for 5–7 minutes until fragrant.
2.Add milk
Pour in milk, stir well, and cook on medium heat until the milk reduces and thickens.
3.Sweeten
Add sugar and crushed cardamom, stirring until dissolved and the mixture thickens further.
4.Add dry fruits
Mix in raisins, cashews, and almonds. Cook for another 5 minutes until glossy.
5.Finish
Serve warm, garnished with pistachios or more nuts of choice.
