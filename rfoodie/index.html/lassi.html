1. Prep the Blender
Add the ice cubes to your blender, followed by the chilled curd. Using cold ingredients right from the start is key to getting a rich and frothy lassi.
2. Add the Flavor
Pour in the sugar and the cardamom powder. The cardamom provides a wonderful, aromatic freshness.
3. Blend
Secure the lid and blend everything until it becomes smooth, rich, and creamy. Avoid adding water for a traditional, thick dhaba-style consistency.
4. Serve
Pour the lassi into tall glasses. Garnish with saffron strands and a sprinkle of finely chopped almonds and pistachios before serving immediately.
