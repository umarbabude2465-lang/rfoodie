1.Prepare sugar syrup
Boil water and sugar together until slightly sticky. Add cardamom and saffron, keep warm.
2.Make dough
Mix grated khoya, flour, and baking powder. Add milk gradually to form a soft dough.
3.Shape balls
Divide dough into small smooth balls without cracks.
4.Fry
Heat ghee/oil on low-medium. Fry balls until golden brown, turning gently.
5.Soak
Immediately transfer fried balls into warm sugar syrup. Let them soak for at least 2 hours.
6.Serve
Serve warm or chilled, garnished with nuts.
